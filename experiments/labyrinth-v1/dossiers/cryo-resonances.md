# Cryosphere / sub-ice resonances

This is a routing dossier, not a leaf. Three instrument signatures exist:

- [Percussive echo](https://github.com/bonoj/Pit/blob/main/experiments/labyrinth-v1/instruments/knock.md): a single knock repeated by canyon geometry.
- [Sustained bell-tone](https://github.com/bonoj/Pit/blob/main/experiments/labyrinth-v1/instruments/bell.md): a bell heard beneath frozen water, with a slow metallic decay.
- [Hydrophone hiss](https://github.com/bonoj/Pit/blob/main/experiments/labyrinth-v1/instruments/hiss.md): continuous granular noise under ice.
